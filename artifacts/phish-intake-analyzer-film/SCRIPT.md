# Phish Intake Analyzer — Project Film

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** Turn an uncertain message into a reviewable, evidence-first triage note.
- **Identity:** The existing app's warm paper, deep slate, sea-glass green, and restrained rust; Space Grotesk display, DM Sans support, DM Mono technical labels.
- **Hero:** A suspicious parcel SMS becoming a structured risk assessment.
- **Motion:** Message text resolves into a magnified URL; the URL branches into evidence; evidence assembles into a scored report.
- **Arc:** Intrigue → scrutiny → synthesis → measured human conclusion.
- **Payoff:** The opening URL, initially a threat, becomes one small piece of a broader evidence ledger.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.
- **Runtime:** 24 seconds.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.55s / 4.55s — “A MESSAGE WITH A HOOK”
**Purpose:** Make the suspicious message instantly recognizable and establish the exact intake subject.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Deep slate canvas with a huge, slightly rotated paper-white SMS transcript card, cropped at the left and bottom. Green sender metadata occupies the upper-left safe zone; a rust-red link fragment cuts across the center. The message card behaves like physical evidence under an analyst's desk lamp: a tight pool of warm light and a faint ruled-paper grid distinguish it from generic UI.

**Exact content:** 0.00s: `UNKNOWN SENDER` in 2vmin mono at x=7%, y=13%. 0.22s: `Your package is on hold` appears in 6.5vmin Space Grotesk. 0.70s: `Unpaid $1.82 customs fee.` stamps in below at 4vmin. 1.20s: `Confirm within 12 hours:` appears at 3vmin. 1.60s: `parcel-update-check.example` appears in 3.4vmin mono, rust underline drawing beneath it. The full visible text is held through 4.20s. No voiceover.

**Choreography:** 0.00–0.22s: sender label and a short green rule snap into view. 0.22–0.70s: first line reveals through a horizontal mask. 0.70–1.20s: the fee punctuates with a stiff scale snap; the card answers with a small tilt recoil. 1.20–1.60s: deadline copy enters by line-mask. 1.60–2.15s: URL types in as three short chunks with distinct 0.15s gaps; underline traces from the suspicious domain's actual baseline. 2.15–3.55s: tight camera push makes the URL the hero, growing to roughly one-third of frame height; surrounding message softens without blur filters. 3.55–4.20s: rust underline detaches, snakes into a path, and sweeps toward center. 4.20–4.55s: URL scales toward lens to 2.8x while text splits upward/downward, initiating the next reveal.

**Handoff:** At 4.55s the animated underline is the physical carrier; it continues as the destination-inspection path. Compound cut: URL push-through plus a two-panel paper field unfolding beneath it. Cut aligned to measured rise at 4.55–4.60s.

**Assets and technique:** No external imagery. Layered CSS paper card, clipped type masks, deterministic SVG underline/path, perspective on the card plane, two-panel split. Existing product UI is source reference; this artwork is a faithful visual abstraction.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental electronic pulse with crisp low-mid rhythm; desired edit accents at 5.0s and opener punctuation at 1.6s. Accent alignment unverified until generated audio is measured.

### Shot 03 / 10.35–16.15s / 5.80s — “EVIDENCE, ASSEMBLED”

Instead of a static dashboard screenshot, three evidence strips physically arrive from different depths and snap into the report's grid. **Exact content:** 10.47s: `TRIAGE ASSESSMENT` in 2vmin mono at x=6%, y=13%. 10.70s: ring reads `72` in 7vmin mono; beneath, `HIGH RISK` in 2.8vmin mono. 11.60s: `Heuristic score · /100` in 2.3vmin. 12.20s: strip `Urgency · customs fee / 12-hour deadline` in 2.6vmin. 13.00s: strip `Indicator · parcel-update-check.example` in 2.6vmin. 14.00s: findings stack reveals `Link destination mismatch` and `Unusual urgency language` in 2.5vmin. 15.10s: authentication mini-row appears as `SPF  PASS` / `DKIM  PASS` / `DMARC  FAIL` in 2.4vmin, labeled `EMAIL CONTEXT` in 1.9vmin; this is representative report capability, not the SMS's own result. 15.70s: a paper note tab enters with `Review alongside your incident-response process.` in 2.4vmin.

**Choreography:** 10.35–10.70s: ring scales from 1.55x and sharpens while report panels fold open on a 3D hinge. 10.70–11.60s: score arc traces clockwise to 72%, then numeric readout ticks through 18 → 43 → 72 at 0.12s intervals. 11.60–12.20s: score caption types in under the ring. 12.20–13.00s: first evidence strip slides in from depth (not screen edge) and locks. 13.00–14.00s: indicator strip follows; the domain is briefly highlighted in rust then returns to its normal ink. 14.00–15.10s: two findings appear one at a time, each attached to a thin evidence rule. 15.10–15.70s: auth mini-row unfolds on the card's lower edge. 15.70–15.95s: human-review note enters as a paper tab. 15.95–16.15s: report slab and ring scale toward lens; content layers shear apart as ring persists across the cut.

**Handoff:** At 16.15s score ring becomes a thin mint outline on slate; report panels collapse into one flat analyst note. Compound cut: ring push-through plus hinged paper planes collapsing and linework carrying into the next scene. Cut aligned to measured rise at 16.15–16.20s.

**Assets and technique:** CSS report slab, SVG score arc, perspective hinges, evidence rules, safe-frame type column, no external imagery. The `72` and auth checks are illustrative film graphics, not a claim about a specific live analysis.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental lift around the score resolve at 10.85s and evidence sequence; desired cut at 17.0s, pending measured onsets.
